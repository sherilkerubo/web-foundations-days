About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight, distraction-free web application designed to help you jot down fleeting thoughts instantly. It keeps your workspace clean so you can focus entirely on what matters most: your ideas.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Navigate to the home page.
  2. Type your idea into the new note input field.
  3. Click the add button or press enter to save it to your list.

Features

  • Instant local note capture
  • Clean, semantic markup
  • Accessible form elements and keyboard navigation
